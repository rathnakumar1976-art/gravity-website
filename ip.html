Intellectual property

Gravity Infotainment develops original ideas and protects them. Our work on global timekeeping is the subject of two patent applications in India, both under examination at the Indian Patent Office, and of two registered copyrights.

Patent applications

Both applications are pending. Neither has been granted yet.

  • Global Time Protocol (GTP)

    A hierarchical, decimal protocol for keeping one global time, in which every moment has a single timestamp that is the same everywhere on Earth.

    Patent pending, India

  • Global Time digital clock

    A digital clock device based on the Global Time Protocol.

    Patent pending, India

Registered copyrights

Two works are registered with the Copyright Office, Government of India, under the Copyright Act, 1957.

  • Global Time - Digital Clock

    A work describing the Global Time digital clock, based on the Global Time Protocol.

    Registered copyright, India, 2025

  • Global Communication Protocol (GCP)

    A literary work on a protocol for global communication, covering real-time synchronisation, time stamping and platform integration.

    Registered copyright, India, 2024
