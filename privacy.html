Privacy policy

This policy explains what information this website collects about visitors, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 7 October 2026.

  • Who we are

    This website, gravityinfotainment.com, is run by Gravity Infotainment, a sole proprietorship of Rathna Kumar, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: developer@gravityinfotainment.com.

  • Your choice comes first

    We collect visitor statistics only if you click Accept on the cookie notice. If you decline, or do nothing, no analytics tools are loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

  • What we collect if you accept

    Pages you view, how long you stay, what you click and how far you scroll, the type of device and browser you use, the site you came from, and your approximate location such as city and country, worked out from your internet address.

  • Session replays and heatmaps

    Microsoft Clarity records how visitors move through the pages, including mouse movement, clicks and scrolling, so we can see where the site is confusing. These recordings do not identify you by name.

  • Age, gender and interests

    Google Analytics may show us estimated age groups, gender and interest categories for our visitors as a whole. These are Google's estimates for groups of people. We cannot see them for any individual.

  • Why we collect it

    To understand which pages are useful, to fix problems, and to improve the site. We do not sell this information and we do not use it to advertise to you.

  • Who processes it

    The statistics are processed for us by Google (Google Analytics) and Microsoft (Microsoft Clarity), under their own privacy terms. They use cookies and similar storage in your browser to tell one visit from another.

  • The Global Time demo

    The demo keeps your settings, such as chosen cities and alarms, in your own browser so they are there on your next visit. That information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

  • When you contact us

    If you email or phone us, we keep your message and contact details for as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

  • Your rights

    You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. Write to the email address above and we will respond.

  • Children

    This website is meant for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

  • Changes

    If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Each of our apps will have its own privacy policy when it is released.
